# Risk of Rain 2: Meta Progression Without Stat Upgrades

Context: Hopoo Games released RoR2 in early access in 2019 and 1.0 in 2020. Gearbox bought the Risk of Rain IP in November 2022 but did not buy the studio. Hopoo said it would make non-RoR games independently ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/133213/gearbox-entertainment-risk-of-rain); [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/gearbox-acquires-risk-of-rain-franchise-from-hopoo-games)). Gearbox developed and 2K published the later DLCs:
- Seekers of the Storm (2024)
- Alloyed Collective (18 Nov 2025) ([2K patch notes](https://support.2k.com/hc/en-us/articles/46614659423891-Risk-of-Rain-2-Alloyed-Collective-and-Patch-Notes-November-18-2025); [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/risk-of-rain-2/alloyed-collective-dlc-launch))
- Hallowed Concepts (8 Oct 2026) ([Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/risk-of-rain-2-dlc-hallowed-concepts-now-available))

As a result, statements about the original design intent come from Hopoo. Later systems, such as the Lunar Coin rewards for challenges, are Gearbox changes.

## Challenges (achievements): how many, what they unlock, design examples

### Takeaway
RoR2's whole persistent layer is about 200 challenges. Each one unlocks a survivor, an alternate skill, a skin, an item or equipment piece added to the drop pool, or an artifact. None of them gives a permanent stat bonus. The challenges ask for varied skill or exploration feats (speed, no-hit, finding secrets, Monsoon clears). They are not grind counters, though a few cumulative ones exist (e.g. 3,000 kills).

By category: Survivors 14, Items 30, Equipment 14, Skills 65, Skins 62, Artifacts 20. ### Inferences
- About 30 items and 14 equipment start locked (going by the challenge tables). Against about 232 items and 46 equipment, that means roughly 13% of items and about 30% of equipment start locked. Most of the item pool is available on a fresh install. Unlocks widen the pool; they do not raise power.
- Challenge design leans on teaching. Early unlocks reward basic actions such as finishing a teleporter, dying 5 times, or finding 10 whites. Mid unlocks reward specific mastery or secrets. Late unlocks reward Monsoon clears. Dying itself is rewarded ("Learning Process", "I Love Dying!"), which softens failure.

### Gaps
- The wiki does not split the challenge count by DLC. The 205 total and the per-category counts come from a fetch-tool summary of a very long page, not a manual count.
- Survivor challenges for Railgunner, Seeker and Operator did not appear in the extract. Enforcer is reportedly available from the start ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/risk-of-rain-2-what-acolyte-and-enforcer-do-in-hallowed-concepts/)).
- I found no official number for how many base-game (pre-DLC) challenges existed at 1.0.

## Survivors and alternate skills/loadouts

### Takeaway
There are 20 selectable survivors in the base game plus four DLCs, and 2 are available at the start. Most of the rest unlock through feats or secrets. About 65 alternate-skill challenges change how a survivor plays, giving horizontal variety rather than vertical power.

### Cited Findings
- The wiki lists 20 selectable survivors, plus Heretic, a special transformation reached "through extraordinary means." Commando and Huntress are available from the start. — [wiki.gg: Survivors](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivors)
- Survivors by source — [wiki.gg: Survivors](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivors):

  | Source | Survivors |
  |---|---|
  | Base game | Commando, Huntress, Bandit, MUL-T, Engineer, Artificer, Mercenary, REX, Loader, Acrid, Captain (11) |
  | Survivors of the Void | Railgunner, Void Fiend |
  | Seekers of the Storm | Seeker, False Son, CHEF |
  | Alloyed Collective | Operator, Drifter |
  | Hallowed Concepts | Enforcer, Acolyte |
- Alloyed Collective added 2 survivors, 6 stages, 7 drones, 18 items and 6 enemies. — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/risk-of-rain-2/alloyed-collective-dlc-launch)
- The Loadout system lets players "mix and match abilities before a run." — [wiki.gg: Survivors](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivors)
- The Challenges page lists 65 skill-unlock challenges and 62 skin-unlock challenges. — [wiki.gg: Challenges](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Challenges)

### Inferences
- With about 65 alt-skill unlocks across about 20 survivors (an average of about 3 each), the bulk of RoR2's unlocks are playstyle options. Examples include Engineer's Spider Mines versus Pressure Mines and Mercenary's aerial Rising Thunder.
- Several survivor unlocks require finding a hidden location (Void Fields, Siren's Call, the Fuel Array quest, the Obelisk). Discovery takes the place of currency.

### Gaps
- I found no exact count of skill slots per survivor or of default versus locked alternates, and no developer commentary on alt-skill design in this pass.

## Artifacts

### Takeaway
Artifacts are opt-in run modifiers, some harder and some sillier. They are unlocked by finding 9-symbol codes hidden in the world, entering them at Sky Meadow's Compound Generator, and beating a trial in Bulwark's Ambry. There are 20 artifacts in the current list, including DLC ones.

### Cited Findings
- Unlock flow:
  1. Codes are 9 symbols (■●▲♦) found on stone tablets in the world.
  2. Enter the code at the Compound Generator in Sky Meadow (stage 5). With Seekers of the Storm, a laptop in Helminth Hatchery also works.
  3. This opens a portal to Bulwark's Ambry.
  4. Pick up Artifact Keys, activate the Artifact Reliquary, and fight waves with the trial artifact active.
  5. Destroy the Reliquary to unlock the artifact.

  In multiplayer, unlocks are pooled across the lobby. — [wiki.gg: Artifacts](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Artifacts)
- Once unlocked, an artifact can be toggled on at the start of any run. In multiplayer, any artifact unlocked by any member can be used. — [wiki.gg: Artifacts](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Artifacts)
- Full list — [wiki.gg: Artifacts](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Artifacts):

  | Artifact | Effect |
  |---|---|
  | Chaos | Friendly fire |
  | Command | Choose your items via Command Essences |
  | Death | One dies, all die |
  | Delusion | Chests reclose after the teleporter for a memory test; a wrong pick costs a stack |
  | Devotion | Drones become Lemurian eggs and followers that share offered items |
  | Dissonance | Monsters from other environments appear |
  | Enigma | Random equipment that changes on use |
  | Evolution | Monsters gain items between stages |
  | Frailty | Fall damage doubled and lethal |
  | Glass | 500% damage, 10% HP |
  | Honor | Elites only |
  | Kin | One monster type per stage |
  | Metamorphosis | Random survivor each spawn |
  | Prestige (Alloyed Collective) | At least one Shrine of the Mountain per stage, effects permanent |
  | Rebirth (Seekers of the Storm) | Start with a "gift from a previous life"; no code |
  | Sacrifice | Monsters drop items, no chests |
  | Soul | Wisps from corpses |
  | Spite | Bombs on death |
  | Swarms | Double spawns, half monster HP |
  | Vengeance | Doppelganger every 10 minutes |
- Artifact unlocks are also tracked as challenges (20 in the Artifacts category), e.g. "Trial of Chaos" (3 coins). Rebirth unlocks by making an offering at the Shrine of Rebirth ("Experienced Rebirth", 2 coins). — [wiki.gg: Challenges](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Challenges)

### Inferences
- Artifacts work like a player-chosen modifier deck. Some make the game harder or stranger (Honor, Frailty, Death); others give more control or power (Command, Sacrifice). Players can tune the experience without the game ever increasing base stats.
- Rebirth is the only artifact that carries something from one run into the next. It is notable as a Gearbox-era step toward cross-run carryover, though it is opt-in.

### Gaps
- Exact mechanics of Rebirth's "gift" (which item carries over and under what rules) were not captured. The wiki page was truncated.

## Lunar Coins and the Bazaar Between Time

### Takeaway
Lunar Coins are the only currency that persists between runs. They are spent on run-scoped things: Lunar items (high-risk, high-reward), shop rerolls, choosing the next stage, Bazaar access, and Artificer's one-time unlock. They never buy permanent stat increases.

### Cited Findings
- Coins persist across runs; gold does not. — [wiki.gg: Lunar Coin](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Lunar_Coin)
- Drop rate from kills: each enemy kill has a 0.5% chance to drop a coin. Each drop halves the chance for the rest of the run (0.25%, 0.125%, ...), and it resets each run. — [wiki.gg: Lunar Coin](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Lunar_Coin)
- Fixed sources — [wiki.gg: Lunar Coin](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Lunar_Coin):
  - Obliterating at the Obelisk: 5
  - Twisted Scavenger in A Moment, Whole: 10 (15 total with the Obelisk)
  - Defeating Mithrix and escaping: 10
  - Defeating the Voidling (SotV): 10
  - First completion of a challenge: 1/2/3/5/10
- Spending — [wiki.gg: Bazaar Between Time](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Bazaar_Between_Time); pod costs from [wiki.gg: Lunar Coin](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Lunar_Coin):

  | Purchase | Cost |
  |---|---|
  | Newt Altar (guarantees the Bazaar blue portal; natural chance is 5% / (portals opened + 1)) | 1 coin |
  | Lunar Bud in the Bazaar (5 random Lunar items, each buyable once; only items some player has unlocked) | 2 coins |
  | Shop reroll | 1, then 2, 4, 8, 16... (doubling) |
  | Lunar Seer (pick the next environment from up to 2) | 3 coins |
  | Lunar Pod outside the Bazaar | 1 coin |
  | Artificer unlock | 10 coins (one time) |

  Cauldrons trade items with no coin cost (3 commons → 1 uncommon; 5 uncommons → 1 legendary).
- Minion kills can now drop coins on the player's behalf (an early-access "Skills 2.0" change). Coins were once whole numbers and were later shown fractionally. — [wiki.gg: Lunar Coin](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Lunar_Coin)

### Inferences
- Earn rate per run, estimated from the rules above rather than a sourced figure: kills give only a few coins per run because of the halving, roughly 1 coin per 200 kills at first, then 1 per 400. Boss clears add 10 to 15 each. A full win with an Obelisk detour lands around 10 to 20 coins, plus challenge payouts.
- Coins work as a persistent "access token" to optional risky content. Lunar items carry heavy drawbacks. This is a meta currency that buys options and variance, not power.

### Gaps
- I found no community-measured average coins per run.
- Exact patch history of the drop-rate and halving rules was not captured, nor whether coins are per-profile or cloud-synced across platforms.

## Difficulty and Eclipse as the long-tail challenge track

### Takeaway
Three base difficulties (Drizzle, Rainstorm, Monsoon) gate Mastery skins and some unlocks. Eclipse 1 to 8 is a per-survivor ladder of stacking modifiers built on Monsoon. Its only reward is cosmetic status: a Gold Eclipse icon.

### Cited Findings
- Eclipse modifiers stack cumulatively — [wiki.gg: Eclipse](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Eclipse):

  | Level | Adds |
  |---|---|
  | E1 | Allies start each stage and spawn at 50% HP |
  | E2 | Teleporter/holdout zone radius −50% |
  | E3 | Fall damage +100% and lethal |
  | E4 | Monster movement speed +40% |
  | E5 | Ally healing −50% |
  | E6 | Enemy gold drops −20% |
  | E7 | Monster skill cooldowns halved |
  | E8 | Taking damage adds Permanent Curse stacks (40 × damage/max HP, rounded down; −1% max HP each; resets per stage; hits under 2.5% max HP add none) |
- Each survivor starts at E1 and advances one level per successful run, up to E8. In multiplayer, the lobby's level is the lowest among its survivors. — [wiki.gg: Eclipse](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Eclipse)
- Eclipse uses Monsoon's base difficulty, so Monsoon-required challenges can be completed on Eclipse. Eclipse has "no challenges or unlocks." Clearing E8 on every survivor only grants a Gold Eclipse icon on character select. — [wiki.gg: Eclipse](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Eclipse)
- Monsoon gates: "The Calm" (beat the game on Monsoon → Purity), "Deicide" (elite boss on Monsoon → Brainstalks), and every survivor's Mastery skin. — [wiki.gg: Challenges](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Challenges)

### Inferences
- Eclipse is a pure difficulty ladder: 8 levels × about 20 survivors, or about 160 winning runs for full completion. Its reward is tracking and bragging rights, much like Hades' Heat or Slay the Spire's Ascension.

### Gaps
- The wiki excerpt did not confirm how Eclipse is first unlocked (I recall it requires beating the game once, but that is unverified).
- I did not capture Drizzle, Rainstorm and Monsoon scaling numbers (e.g. difficulty-coefficient multipliers) in this pass.

## Explicit absence of permanent stat upgrades: developer statements and RoR1 heritage

### Takeaway
I found no Hopoo quote specific to RoR2 that explicitly rejects permanent stat upgrades. The clearest developer philosophy comes from Paul Morse on the Risk of Rain series (2024, about Returns). He says unlocks should not gate "the fun content," that later items should add complexity rather than raw power, and that a fresh install can win.

### Cited Findings
- Paul Morse (Hopoo co-founder), Game Developer, 11 Apr 2024 — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/risk-of-rain-interview):
  - "We didn't want to gate 'all the fun content' behind unlocks or force the player to earn them over time."
  - "You can absolutely 'win' in Risk of Rain Returns on a fresh install."
  - "items that were unlocked later into the game weren't strictly power upgrades, but oftentimes more complex game mechanics"
  - "We had to think about each item that was unlocked to make sure it didn't replace or make other items obsolete." On adding items: "we were very careful not to dilute the pool."
  - "A very skillful player could still beat the game with the 'worst' or even no items."
  - He describes meta-progression as unlockable characters, abilities and items earned "as the player accomplishes various tasks."
- Hopoo team (unnamed, "a team of three"), Game Developer, 14 Aug 2019 — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/how-moving-from-2d-to-3d-shaped-the-design-of-i-risk-of-rain-2-i-):
  - "skill takes a front seat in being successful in Risk of Rain 2"
  - The intended arc is that the player "started the game with no items and their weakest, and then progressed naturally" within a run.
  - On the clock-based difficulty: "The player never really feels safe."
- Third-party summary (a review, not a developer statement): apart from unlockable characters, abilities and items, RoR2 has no permanent progress, and death means a full restart. — cited via search summary of [GameGrin review](https://cdn.gamegrin.com/reviews/risk-of-rain-2-review)

### Inferences
- RoR1 (2013) used the same model of unlocking items and characters through achievements. Morse's 2024 comments on Returns describe the series-wide philosophy that RoR2 inherits. Progression lives entirely in-run (item stacking against a difficulty clock), and between-run progression only widens options.
- Unlocked items cannot be "worse" than the pool they join, by design. This guards against unlocks diluting the pool, the main risk of item-pool meta progression.

### Gaps
- I found no Duncan Drummond quote specific to RoR2 on permanent upgrades, and no RoR2 postmortem or GDC talk. A Prima Games interview with Paul Morse about RoR2 exists, but the fetch returned 403 ([Prima](https://primagames.com/gaming/risk-of-rain-2-interview-paul-morse)).
- A claim that Hopoo staff moved to Valve in 2023 or 2024 was not verified by any source I retrieved.

## Player-facing outcomes: time to unlock everything, pacing

### Takeaway
HowLongToBeat-derived figures are about 10.5 to 11 h for the "main story" (one win), about 44 h for Main + Extras, and about 130 to 150 h for completionist. Unlocks come thick in the first runs (1-coin "do the basics" challenges) and then thin into secret-hunting and Monsoon or skill feats.

### Cited Findings
- HowLongToBeat-attributed times, all from mirrors because I could not reach HLTB directly; HLTB's own figure appears to be about 145 h completionist:

  | Source | Main | Main + Extras | Completionist |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | [Plat Prices](https://platprices.com/en-bg/game/401-risk-of-rain-2) (attributed to HLTB) | 10.5 h | 44 h | 145 h |
  | [Deku Deals](https://www.dekudeals.com/items/risk-of-rain-2) | 11 h | 44.5 h | 150 h |
  | [Gamers Unchained](https://www.gamersunchained.com/game/xbox/risk-of-rain-2) | — | — | 141 h |
  | [GameSpan](https://gamespan.pages.dev/g/risk-of-rain-2) | — | — | about 130 h for 100% |
- Pacing evidence from challenge requirements — [wiki.gg: Challenges](https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Challenges):
  - Early: complete a teleporter (Armor-Piercing Rounds), die 5 times (Tougher Times), complete 3 stages without dying (Bandit), first teleporter 5 times (MUL-T)
  - Mid: 30 cumulative stages (Engineer), 3,000 kills (Infusion), secrets such as the Aqueduct chamber and the Fuel Array
  - Late: beat the game (Captain), Monsoon clear (Purity and Mastery skins), loop to stage 6 or more (Meat Hook), 20 stages in one run (57 Leaf Clover)

### Inferences
- These completionist times likely predate some or all of Alloyed Collective and Hallowed Concepts, so current 100% times are probably higher. The mirrors give no data date.
- Eclipse 8 on all survivors (about 160 winning runs at about 30 to 60 min each) would on its own run past 100 h. That is consistent with the 130 to 150 h completionist range, if HLTB players count it.

### Gaps
- I found no HLTB submission counts, no date of the data, and no community survey of "runs to unlock all survivors."
- I found no official Steam achievement-completion percentages in this pass.
