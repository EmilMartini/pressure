# Hades (Supergiant, 2020) meta progression, with Hades II deltas

Source note: the Hades Fandom wiki pages (hades.fandom.com) were read in full through a real browser on 2026-11-05. They are community-maintained but reflect the 1.0+ game and are the most complete numeric source. Fextralife's Mirror page has several transcription errors (Boiling Blood listed as 2 ranks, Thick Skin costs garbled), and RPG Site's Pact table predates 1.0 (Extreme Measures 3 ranks, Tight Deadline 2 ranks). The Fandom figures are used below, and conflicts are flagged.

## 1. Mirror of Night: talents, ranks, costs, unlocks, respec

### Takeaway
The Mirror has 12 talent pairs (24 talents). Only one side of each pair can be active, and the two sides are ranked separately. Maxing every talent on both sides costs 35,365 Darkness, plus 65 Chthonic Keys to unlock them. About 59% of that Darkness (21,000) goes to the two "Fated" reroll talents at the bottom, so the core combat power is cheap (~6.4k) and the late cost sits in reroll/agency talents. A full respec costs 1 Key and refunds all spent Darkness.

### Cited Findings
**Structure and rules**
- Darkness is spent at the Mirror. Many abilities start locked and need Chthonic Keys. "Refunding Darkness costs one Chthonic Key and refunds all Darkness spent in the Mirror of Night." This refund unlocks "after several escape attempts". — [Hades Wiki: Mirror of Night](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_of_Night)
- Each ability has a red and a green version. The green versions unlock "following a conversation with Nyx after collecting a minimum of 300 [Darkness]". Each version is invested in separately (e.g. Shadow Presence ranks do not upgrade Fiery Presence). "Only one version of each ability can be active at a time, and they can be switched between freely while reflecting at the Mirror." — [Hades Wiki: Mirror of Night](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_of_Night)
- Key unlock tiers: the top 4 pairs are available from the start. The next tiers of 2 pairs cost 5, 10, 20 and 30 Keys, so "it takes a total of 65 [Keys] to unlock all upgrades on the Mirror." — [Hades Wiki: Mirror of Night](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_of_Night); the 5/10/20/30 batching is corroborated by [Fextralife](https://hades.wiki.fextralife.com/Mirror+of+the+Night)
- History: the A/B flip-side design arrived in Early Access Patch 040 ("The Nighty Night Update", 10 Mar 2020). The patch notes describe the Mirror as "now with twice the Talents, inviting more choices to fit your playstyle" and the rework as "More choices, improved usability, reduced total Darkness cost". In the same patch, ranks could no longer be subtracted one at a time and a full Darkness refund option replaced that. Talents now unlock two rows at a time. Later talents got cheaper, and players who had over-invested were refunded the difference. — [Supergiant patch notes: Nighty Night Update](https://supergiantgames.com/blog/hades-the-nighty-night-update-patch-notes)

**Totals**
- Wiki totals: maxing everything costs 35,365 Darkness. The page says 6,165 for "all non-Boon, non-Fated" talents and 14,365 including the Boon-odds talents. — [Hades Wiki: Mirror of Night](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_of_Night). The 35,365 figure is repeated on [Hades Wiki: Darkness](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness). Fextralife's sum is 35,375 because of its transcription errors ([Fextralife](https://hades.wiki.fextralife.com/Mirror+of+the+Night)).
- Re-adding the per-rank figures in the wiki table gives: red side 18,800, green side 16,565, total 35,365 (matches the wiki). The non-Boon, non-Fated subtotal re-adds to **6,365, not the 6,165 the wiki states**. 6,365 + 8,000 (the four Boon-odds talents) = 14,365, which matches the wiki's next figure, so 6,165 looks like a typo. — derived from [Hades Wiki: Mirror of Night](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_of_Night)
- Fated Authority's dice can turn any room into another reward from the same pool. Blue-laurel doors (Keys, Gemstones, Darkness, Nectar) are one pool; gold-laurel doors (boons, hammers, Obols, Centaur Hearts, Poms) are the other. — [Hades Wiki: Mirror of Night](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_of_Night)
- Interaction with the Pact: the Routine Inspection condition disables Mirror talents 3 at a time from the bottom up (4 ranks, up to 12 talents). — [Hades Wiki: Pact of Punishment](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Pact_of_Punishment); introduced in [Patch 040](https://supergiantgames.com/blog/hades-the-nighty-night-update-patch-notes)

### Inferences
- The cost curve is front-loaded for survival and back-loaded for agency. The first Death Defiance costs 30 Darkness (roughly 3 room rewards), but the 2nd and 3rd cost 500 and 1,000. All combat-relevant talents together cost about 6.4k. The Fated dice (21k, ~59% of the total) are a late sink that buys control over randomness, not raw stats.
- The A/B pairs make the Mirror a loadout, not a ladder. Within a slot you trade safety for damage: Death Defiance x3 vs a per-chamber Stubborn Defiance; +50 HP vs +25% damage while healthy; rerolling rooms vs rerolling boons. Swaps are free, so after the first investment most decisions are about build fit rather than power.
- The 1-Key, full-refund respec keeps early mistakes cheap and encourages experimenting.

### Gaps
- No developer statement found that explicitly explains *why* the A/B variant structure was chosen, beyond the patch-note line "inviting more choices to fit your playstyle".
- The exact number of runs before the refund option appears ("after several escape attempts") was not found.

## 2. Currencies: earn rates, uses, and time to max

### Takeaway
Hades splits progression across 6 permanent currencies, each tied to one sink. Darkness goes to the Mirror. Keys unlock talents and weapons. Gemstones and Diamonds go to the House Contractor. Nectar and Ambrosia go to relationships and keepsakes. Titan Blood goes to weapon aspects. The rare boss currencies (Titan Blood, Diamonds, Ambrosia) are drip-fed mostly through per-weapon, per-Heat bounties: the full set takes 126 successful clears (6 weapons x 21 Heat levels). Reliable runs-to-max-Mirror estimates were not found.

### Cited Findings
**Darkness**
- Sources: room-clear rewards; Infernal Troves; the Fated List of prophecies (1,000 total); the Wretched Broker. When Heat is too low for a bounty, bosses drop Darkness instead: Furies 50, Bone Hydra 100, Theseus and Asterius 150, final boss 250 (550 per full clear). — [Hades Wiki: Darkness](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness)
- Room reward size: 10 Darkness per standard chamber, 20 from skull (harder) chambers. — community guides summarized in search results, e.g. [Hades Wiki: Chambers and Encounters](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Chambers_and_Encounters) (**not fetched directly; treat as medium confidence**)
- Multipliers: the Pitch-Black Darkness contractor upgrade adds +5 per Darkness room reward; a weapon with the "Dark Thirst" bonus gives +20% Darkness that run; Poseidon's Ocean's Bounty boon gives +50-65% Darkness from all sources; companion Shady gives 10 per summon; Sisyphus offers 50-55. — [Hades Wiki: Darkness](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness)
- Sinks: Mirror (35,365); Nyx's favor (3,142); the Resource Director, a post-game ranking with **325,000 Darkness** for all ranks; the Infinite Theme (25,000). — [Hades Wiki: Darkness](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness)
- Patch 028 converted Heat-as-a-resource into Darkness: "5 Darkness for each Heat lost". — [Supergiant: Superstar Update patch notes](https://www.supergiantgames.com/blog/hades-superstar-update-patch-notes/)

**Chthonic Keys**
- 89 Keys are spent on permanent unlocks: 65 at the Mirror and 24 on weapons. A Mirror reset costs 1 Key. Resource Director ranks take 100 Keys; the Chthonic Theme costs 200. With the Fated Keys contractor upgrade, each Key room reward also gives +1 Fated die. — [Hades Wiki: Chthonic Key](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Chthonic_Key)
- Weapon Key costs: Spear 4, Shield 3, Bow 1, Twin Fists 8 (needs the first four weapons), Rail 8 (needs all others) = 24. — [Hades Wiki: Infernal Arms](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Infernal_Arms)
- Gemstone-to-Key rate at the Wretched Broker: 10 Gemstones per Key. — [Hades Wiki: Gemstones](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Gemstones)

**Gemstones** (House Contractor, mostly quality-of-life and cosmetics)
- Room rewards give 5 Gemstones (normal) or 10 (hard). Infernal Troves can be upgraded to give 25, then 50, then 75 (660 Gemstones of upgrades). The Vanquisher's Keep order (4 Diamonds) makes bosses drop 10/20/30/50. — [Hades Wiki: Gemstones](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Gemstones)
- Buying every House Contractor upgrade takes 32,585 Gemstones. Prophecies supply 4,580, leaving 28,005 to farm. — [Hades Wiki: Gemstones](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Gemstones)
- Broker trade-up prices in Gemstones: Key 10, Nectar 50, Diamond 500, Ambrosia 1,000, Titan Blood 1,000. — [Hades Wiki: Gemstones](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Gemstones)

**Diamonds** (higher-tier House Contractor items)
- Sources: first Bone Hydra kill with each weapon (6), Hydra bounties at each Heat up to 20 (120), and prophecies (33), for 159 total. Sinks: work orders 52, decorations 28, music 67, Resource Director 10, for 157 total. Charon's Temple of Styx shop occasionally sells one for 1,000 Obols. — [Hades Wiki: Diamond](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Diamond)

**Nectar and Ambrosia** (relationships and keepsakes)
- The first Nectar given to a character returns a Keepsake. After 5-6 Nectar, the character offers a quest whose completion unlocks the remaining affinity hearts, which take Ambrosia. Total Nectar for keepsakes and affinity: 149. Nectar comes from room rewards, a 5% chance from Troves, and 3 from prophecies. — [Hades Wiki: Nectar](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Nectar)
- Ambrosia comes from the Elysium boss (first clear and bounties). The wiki reportedly gives 126 Ambrosia in total, which is consistent with 6 first clears + 6x20 bounties. — search summary of [Hades Wiki: Ambrosia](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Ambrosia) (**not fetched directly**)

**Titan Blood** (weapon aspects)
- Cost to fully upgrade an aspect: 5 (Zagreus aspect), 15 (2nd), 16 (3rd), 15 (hidden 4th), which is 51 per weapon. All aspects on all 6 weapons take **306 Titan Blood**; adding the Resource Director and the Stygian Theme brings it to 326. — [Hades Wiki: Titan Blood](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Titan_Blood)
- Sources: first clear of the Furies and the final boss with each weapon (12); bounties at each Heat up to 20 (240); prophecies (43). That is 295 from bosses and prophecies, so 11 more are needed from the Broker, Charon, or fishing just to max all aspects. — [Hades Wiki: Titan Blood](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Titan_Blood)
- Aspects become available one run after the Rail is unlocked and at least 1 Titan Blood is held. Hidden aspects are revealed by diegetic story conditions (character conversations), not by currency alone. — [Hades Wiki: Infernal Arms](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Infernal_Arms)

**Boss reward pattern**
- On the first kill with each weapon: Furies give Titan Blood, Bone Hydra a Diamond, Theseus and Asterius Ambrosia, the final boss Titan Blood. A repeat kill with the same weapon, Heat and conditions gives Darkness instead. — [GamePressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/hades/rewards-for-killing-bosses/zfec5b); consistent with [Hades Wiki: Titan Blood](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Titan_Blood)

**Time to max**
- No reliable community estimate of runs or hours to max the Mirror was found. Searches returned only the 35,365 total. — [search attempt: Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1145360/discussions/0/3030299766084095028) had no numbers.
- For all bounty currencies: the bounty limit is 20 Heat, and "21 completed runs ... need to be completed to collect all bounties" per weapon. — [Hades Wiki: Pact of Punishment](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Pact_of_Punishment)

### Inferences
- Rough Darkness math (my estimate, not sourced): a full clear with no bounty gives 550 from bosses, plus maybe ~10-20 Darkness rooms at 10-20 each. That suggests ~600-1,000 Darkness per successful low-Heat clear before multipliers, and much less per failed early run. At those rates the combat core (~6.4k) takes roughly 10-25 runs, and the full 35k Mirror takes very roughly 40-80+ runs. **Treat as order-of-magnitude only.**
- Hades deliberately sets **exclusive currency-to-sink mappings**: Darkness for the Mirror, Titan Blood for aspects, Gemstones and Diamonds for the contractor, Nectar and Ambrosia for people. Each system can be balanced independently, and every room-reward choice (blue laurel) becomes a choice of which track to advance.
- Rare currencies are gated by **successful clears per weapon per Heat level**. This forces weapon variety and steady difficulty increases rather than grinding a favourite weapon on easy settings.
- Post-game sinks (325k-Darkness Resource Director, 200-Key and 25k-Darkness themes) exist mainly for completionists. The power-relevant economy finishes well before them.

### Gaps
- Darkness per room by biome, and the number of Darkness rooms per run, could not be verified from a fetched page.
- No developer-published telemetry on median runs or hours to max the Mirror or aspects.

## 3. Pact of Punishment: conditions, Heat, bounties, nudges

### Takeaway
The Pact unlocks after the first successful escape. It has 15 conditions (16 in Hell Mode) for a maximum of 63 Heat (64 in Hell Mode). Rewards are a per-weapon "Target Heat" ladder: each clear at or above the target pays one rare currency per boss and raises that weapon's target by 1, up to Heat 20 (25 in Hell Mode). Heat replaces a difficulty slider with modular, build-dependent modifiers, and the per-weapon ladder pushes players up in steps of +1.

### Cited Findings
**Unlock and framing**
- The Pact appears only after the final boss is beaten once. In Hell Mode it is available from the start and cannot be fully disabled. The in-game notice reads: "While your Heat Gauge is full, you can earn valuable Bounties! ... Once you successfully escape, you can earn more Bounties... if you turn up the Heat"; and "How much Heat you need is per-weapon ... If things get too hot, try switching weapons." — [Hades Wiki: Pact of Punishment](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Pact_of_Punishment)

- Gating: Extreme Measures is hidden until all three Furies have been met, and its Rank 4 must be bought from the House Contractor after the main game is completed. Routine Inspection is hidden until the Mirror is fully unlocked. — [Hades Wiki: Pact of Punishment](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Pact_of_Punishment)
- Conflicting older sources: RPG Site lists Extreme Measures at 3 ranks (1/2/2) and Tight Deadline at 2 ranks (2/3), and omits Personal Liability. That is a pre-1.0 snapshot. — [RPG Site](https://rpgsite.net/feature/10287-hades-pact-of-punishment-heat-modifiers-and-how-to-maximize-your-rewards)

**Bounties and rewards**
- Each boss slain at or above the weapon's Target Heat drops its bounty (Titan Blood, Diamond or Ambrosia). Beating the final boss raises that weapon's target. Targets are tracked per weapon. The bounty limit is 20 Heat (21 runs per weapon); in Hell Mode bounties start at 5 and run to 25. — [Hades Wiki: Pact of Punishment](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Pact_of_Punishment)
- "You can only claim rewards for the lowest Heat you have not yet completed, even if you defeat a boss at a higher heat". Jumping ahead does not skip rungs, and Heat-0 bounties are tracked separately. — [Hades Wiki: Pact of Punishment](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Pact_of_Punishment). (RPG Site's claim that skipping forfeits lower-Heat rewards is contradicted by the wiki and by a [Steam forum player](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1145360/discussions/0/3189112650406109714); the wiki rule appears correct.)
- Totals across bounties: 240 Titan Blood (2 bosses x 6 weapons x 20 Heat) — [Titan Blood](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Titan_Blood); 120 Diamonds — [Diamond](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Diamond); Ambrosia presumably 120 by the same pattern (inferred).
- Other Heat rewards: beating the final boss at 8, 16 and 32 Heat unlocks cosmetic Skelly statues. 5, 10 and 15 Heat open the Infernal Gates to Erebus in Tartarus, Asphodel and Elysium. — [Hades Wiki: Pact of Punishment](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Pact_of_Punishment)
- Prophecy nudge: "Harsh Conditions" asks for a clear with each condition (one rank each, any weapon, across runs). — [Hades Wiki: Pact of Punishment](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Pact_of_Punishment)

**History of the Pact**
- Patch 028 (Superstar Update, 8 Oct 2019) "Overhauled the system; now linked to Bounties". Heat was rescaled ("1 unit now is equivalent to about 5 units before") and stopped being a collectible resource. — [Supergiant: Superstar Update notes](https://www.supergiantgames.com/blog/hades-superstar-update-patch-notes/)
- Before Superstar, every Heat point gave +2% Darkness, there were no bounties, and the condition unlocks needed 200 Heat plus 80 Skeleton Keys. When first introduced the Pact had "no rewards (aside from bragging rights)". — [Hades Wiki: Pact of Punishment](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Pact_of_Punishment)

**Hell Mode**
- Hell Mode is chosen at file creation, cannot be changed, and disables God Mode. It starts with 1 mandatory rank in each of Hard Labor, Lasting Consequences, Jury Summons, Calisthenics Program and Personal Liability (5 Heat). — [Hades Wiki: Hell Mode](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Hell_Mode)
- Practical ceiling: GamesRadar reported that top players say a single attempt at max Heat takes "at least 85 hours". — [GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/hades-highest-difficulty-is-so-tough-that-top-players-say-a-single-attempt-takes-at-least-85-hours-to-pull-off) (headline only, **not fetched**)

### Inferences
- Bounties are capped at 20 while the Pact goes to 63. Heat beyond 20 has no currency payoff, only statues (32) and self-set challenge. Rewards cover the "normal" mastery band, and the rest is intrinsic.
- Per-weapon targets plus "lowest uncompleted rung" make +1 Heat per clear the efficient path. The wiki also says that if Heat gets too high you should switch weapons. Together these turn difficulty into a gentle, self-paced ladder that also forces players to rotate through the weapon roster.
- Routine Inspection directly cancels Mirror power. The meta-progression can be partly switched back off as a Heat source, so a fully upgraded player can recover a "fair fight".

### Gaps
- No official statement of the Heat curve used for balance targets (e.g. intended win rate per Heat).

## 4. How much power the Mirror adds; God Mode as a separate track

### Takeaway
A maxed red-side Mirror roughly doubles Zagreus's effective HP pool and adds three full revives. Most other talents are conditional damage bonuses (+25% to +75% in specific situations) and odds shifts. God Mode is an entirely separate, toggleable damage-resistance ramp from 20% to 80% that does not touch progression.

### Cited Findings
- Max-rank contributions (from the table in section 1): Death Defiance gives 3 revives at 50% HP; Thick Skin +50 max HP; Shadow Presence +50% backstab damage; Fiery Presence +75% vs undamaged foes; Boiling Blood +50% vs Bloodstoned foes; Privileged Status +40% vs doubly-cursed foes; High Confidence +25% while at >=80% HP; Family Favorite +5% per Olympian (e.g. +20-25% with 4-5 gods); Olympian Favor +40% Rare; Gods' Pride +20% Epic; Gods' Legacy +10% Legendary/Duo; Dark Foresight +20% Gold Laurel rooms. — [Hades Wiki: Mirror of Night](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_of_Night)
- Kasavin: "The Mirror of Night is there to make you meaningfully more powerful from one playthrough to a next, so that through a combination of your own growing knowledge and experience, plus your character becoming stronger, you could get farther." — [Tom's Guide interview, 5 Sep 2021](https://www.tomsguide.com/features/hades-exclusive-interview-supergiant)
- God Mode: "can be toggled at any time from the menu on a non-Hell Mode file", grants 20% damage resistance, "increasing by 2% each time a run ends in death (rather than escape)", and caps at 80%. If you turn it off you keep the accumulated percentage for when you turn it back on. It does not lock achievements or content, and runs are recorded separately. — [Hades Wiki: God Mode](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/God_Mode) (citing [Supergiant tweet](https://twitter.com/SupergiantGames/status/1309542802196324353))
- From 20%, the 80% cap is reached after 30 deaths (my arithmetic: (80-20)/2).
- God Mode was added in a December 2019 Early Access update. — [Inverse interview](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/hades-god-mode-interview)

### Inferences
- Taking Zagreus's starting health as 50 (common knowledge, **not verified from a fetched source here**), Thick Skin alone doubles max HP. Combined with 3 Death Defiances, a maxed defensive Mirror gives roughly 4x the effective HP pool of a fresh file. Survivability is the main early Mirror effect, and damage bonuses are conditional, which keeps them build-dependent.
- God Mode scales with failure, not investment. It is a pity timer that sits orthogonal to the Mirror, so struggling players get a guaranteed path to the story without the Mirror or Heat being retuned around them.

### Gaps
- No official number for the starting Life Total was fetched. No developer data on how much the Mirror changes win rates.

## 5. Developer intent: A/B variants, sidegrades, Heat vs difficulty slider, narrative retention

### Takeaway
Supergiant's stated principles are "every run counts", many permanent-progression channels, death as a natural, narratively rewarded beat, no addictive retention hooks, and difficulty options built around the game's own structure. The Pact was explicitly inspired by Slay the Spire's Ascension. Direct statements on *why* the Mirror uses A/B pairs are limited to patch-note language about choice and playstyle.

### Cited Findings
- "Our mantra from the start was 'every run counts,' ... We specifically decided that we would have many channels of permanent progression, as well as an ongoing narrative that would evolve from one playthrough to the next. We wanted there to be no sense of a 'game over' moment, but rather for player deaths to feel like a natural part of the structure." — Greg Kasavin, [Tom's Guide, 2021](https://www.tomsguide.com/features/hades-exclusive-interview-supergiant)
- "By focusing on story and adding more elements of permanent progression, we tried to make sure the moment of dying and restarting was as interesting as possible." — Kasavin, [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/features/hades-exclusive-interview-supergiant)
- On the Pact: Slay the Spire's "brilliantly structured endgame via its Ascension system, which inspired our Pact of Punishment system". Ascension "provides structured challenges to keep you invested long after your first successful clear." — Kasavin, [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/features/hades-exclusive-interview-supergiant)
- Anti-addiction stance: "We wanted the game to be engrossing but were not interested in making it addictive. We avoided keeping players glued, with techniques like daily login bonuses." The story has resolution points so players can "happily walk away". — Kasavin, [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/features/hades-exclusive-interview-supergiant)
- On the difficulty paradox: "Roguelikes have to kill you off so you can start over ... but dying and losing all your progress can also be frustrating enough to make you not want to start over! So it's really important for roguelikes to ... reward the player's time somehow." — Kasavin, [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/features/hades-exclusive-interview-supergiant)
- "It was an explicit goal of our early development, to take the pain out of dying and having to restart"; "You have to be compelled to explore further and feel the time you spent wasn't a waste of your time"; "Reactivity has always been a goal of our narrative design." — Kasavin, [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/how-supergiant-weaves-narrative-rewards-into-i-hades-i-cycle-of-perpetual-death)
- On God Mode and easy modes: "If you could just blow through it, what's interesting about the game goes away"; "What if we just make you a little bit tougher?"; "God Mode reinforces our belief that the way to approach difficulty settings may need to be proprietary to the game." — Kasavin, [Inverse](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/hades-god-mode-interview)
- Lineage: the Pact is "the equivalent to the difficulty functions in earlier Supergiant games - Bastion's shrines, Transistor's limiters, and Pyre's Titan Stars". — [Hades Wiki: Pact of Punishment](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Pact_of_Punishment) (wiki editorial, not a dev quote)
- Mirror A/B rationale (closest primary source): "now with twice the Talents, inviting more choices to fit your playstyle"; "More choices, improved usability, reduced total Darkness cost". — [Patch 040 notes](https://supergiantgames.com/blog/hades-the-nighty-night-update-patch-notes)
- Noclip's six-part "Developing Hell" documentary followed Early Access from Dec 2018, with Amir Rao and Kasavin narrating; episode 2 covers patching. — [GameBanshee ep. 2](https://gamebanshee.com/k4y4h), [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/full-hades-documentary-from-noclip-is-now-available/) (**transcripts not reviewed; no Mirror/Pact quotes extracted**)

### Inferences
- The Pact keeps a single run "fair" by default while giving experts a self-tuned challenge. It is diegetic: the contract is framed as Hades punishing his son, and modular conditions hit builds differently. A difficulty slider would offer neither.
- God Mode (accessibility, failure-driven) and Heat (mastery, success-driven) are two independent axes. Neither changes the Mirror, so the permanent power curve stays the same for all players.

### Gaps
- No primary-source quote found that explains the A/B pairs as deliberate sidegrades, or that says why Heat replaced a slider. GDC 2021 (Korb/Kasavin, dialogue) and the GDC Podcast ep. 16 were not checked in full. Noclip transcripts were not available.

## 6. Hades II: where the approach changed (brief)

### Takeaway
Hades II replaces the Mirror's fixed A/B slots with a **budgeted loadout**: 25 Arcana cards, each with a Grasp cost, under a Grasp cap of 10 rising to 30, against 56 total Grasp of cards. It replaces the Pact's per-boss currency bounties with **Testaments**: specific guardian + weapon + Fear targets that pay Nightmare for aspect upgrades. Kasavin frames the sequel around player-set goals, where failure isn't failure.

### Cited Findings
- Arcana: 25 cards on a 5x5 grid (9 visible at first; buying a card reveals its neighbours). Cards are unlocked mainly with Ashes, and activating one costs 0-5 Grasp. The Grasp limit starts at 10 and rises via Psyche to 29, with 30 needing extra materials. All cards together cost 56 Grasp, so you can never equip everything. There are 6 presets. 0-cost cards activate only through special "awakening" conditions (e.g. Judgement: "Activate no more than 3 cards total"). — [Hades Wiki: Arcana Cards](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Arcana_Cards)
- Death Defiance moves into a card: "Death" gives +1/+2/+3/+4 Death Defiance. Boon-rarity odds become cards too (Excellence, The Queen for Duos, Divinity for Epics). — [Hades Wiki: Arcana Cards](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Arcana_Cards)
- Oath of the Unseen: unlocks after Chronos is first defeated. Vows add Fear. Testaments ask you to beat a named Guardian with a named weapon at a Fear threshold (1, 2, 4, 8, 10, 12, 16, 20 per weapon) and pay Nightmare: 1 at Fear 1-8, 2 at 10-12, 3 at 16-20. The wiki says 16 Vows; Vow of Rivals (12 Fear across 4 ranks) is gated behind boss kills and incantations. — [Hades Wiki: Oath of the Unseen](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Oath_of_the_Unseen)
- Cosmetic statues at 6 Surface + 8 Underworld, 12 + 16, and 24 + 32 Fear (Hades used 8/16/32 Heat). — [Hades Wiki: Oath of the Unseen](https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Oath_of_the_Unseen)
- Kasavin to Edge (via GamesRadar, 20 May 2024): Hades had a "clear goal" but "players started to create a lot of other goals as they played", so Hades II offers "more opportunities to set your own goals"; "If you don't finish the run, it doesn't mean that you failed"; "[we] don't want the game to dictate those goals." — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/hades/hades-2-director-challenges-roguelike-convention-if-you-dont-finish-the-run-it-doesnt-mean-that-you-failed)
- Coverage describes Arcana as replacing the Mirror with a "tarot-like deck ... rearranged between runs". — [DLCompare](https://www.dlcompare.com/gaming-news/hades-ii-official-release-the-perfect-roguelike-formula) (secondary)

### Inferences
- The shift is from "two options per slot, all slots on" (Mirror) to "spend a budget across a grid" (Arcana). The sidegrade principle gets stronger, because power at max progression is still capped by Grasp, and loadouts are meant to be built per weapon.
- Bounties change from a flat ladder (any boss, any rung) to authored, weapon-locked challenges with escalating payouts.

### Gaps
- Re-adding the listed Vow maxima gives 67 Fear across 17 named vows, which conflicts with the page's "16 Vows" count. Max Fear is unverified.
- No Supergiant statement found on why Grasp or the card grid replaced the Mirror pairs.
